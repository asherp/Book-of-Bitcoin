WikiLeaks' public donation address, opened on 14 June 2011, after the banking blockade: Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and the banks had cut the organization off, and bitcoin became the way through. It is the donation stream Satoshi had asked the project to hold off on — "the heat you would bring" — in one of his last posts. It arrived anyway, six months later, and has run ever since: thousands of gifts deep, the longest record on this shelf.

What the chain shows is coins arriving at a script, over fourteen years, from thousands of senders. That the script is WikiLeaks' is a claim, and an unusually well-founded one: the organization published the address itself, which is the standard every ledger here is kept to. It is still a claim, and it is signed below like any other.
