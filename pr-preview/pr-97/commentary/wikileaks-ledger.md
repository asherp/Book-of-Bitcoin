WikiLeaks' public donation address, opened on 14 June 2011 after Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and the banks cut the organization off. Satoshi had asked the project to hold off on bitcoin donations — "the heat you would bring" — in one of his last posts; the address arrived six months later anyway and has collected ever since: thousands of payments, the longest record on this shelf.

What the chain shows is coins arriving at a script, over fourteen years, from thousands of senders. That the script is WikiLeaks' is a claim — a well-founded one, since the organization published the address itself, which is the standard every ledger here is kept to. It is still a claim, and it is signed below like any other.
