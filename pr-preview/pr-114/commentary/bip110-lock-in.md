Lock-in settles the outcome in advance: from this block it is fixed that the rule will bind at the activation height, and nothing else changes yet. No transaction after this chapter is read differently for it; no script becomes invalid; no output changes shape. The chapters between lock-in and activation are the last written under the old terms, and the interval is the design — everyone writing transactions gets a known number of blocks to stop writing what will soon be refused.
