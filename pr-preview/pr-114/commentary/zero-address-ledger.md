The address whose key-hash is twenty bytes of zero — the shortest legible address on the chain, because base58 writes leading zeros as ones. Nothing is known to hash to zero, and no way of finding a key for a chosen hash exists, so the address is keyless by construction and transparently so: a burn address a reader can verify by looking at it, which is why protocols that must burn in public choose it. Blockstack burned its namespace-registration fees here by rule (its SIP-001 names the address); softwares' bugs land here too, a zeroed variable becoming a zero hash becoming an address that swallows the payment.

The ledger is the chain's default incinerator, and it reads at scale: by this book's reading (2 August 2026), 809.42500016 ₿ across 390,869 arrivals and not one departure. The largest single arrival is in the contents as its own entry — the 107 ₿ burned across five sections of one chapter on 25 May 2026, flagged the next day by the analyst Sani of Timechainindex, motive still unargued by evidence. A 2025 survey (arXiv:2503.14057) counts 3,197.61 ₿ provably burned across 7,905 burn addresses, over 99% of it in three ledgers; this shelf now holds all three.
