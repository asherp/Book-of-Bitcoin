I β75, closing 31 October 2011: the deepest cut of the chain's first decade, and the first bubble's aftermath. June 2011 had taken the price to about $32 and the headlines with it; by Halloween it sat under $3, and the miners the bubble had drawn were unplugging. The last entry a book can cite — from Volume II on, retarget windows straddle two books and no book names them.
