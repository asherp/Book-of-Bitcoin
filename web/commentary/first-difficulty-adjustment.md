Sixteen windows in, the target moves for the first time (30 December 2009): nBits 1d00ffff → 1d00d86a, difficulty 1 → about 1.18. The entry cites the window that earned the change rather than the chapter where the new target first bound — a retarget is computed from the 2,016 blocks behind it, and a book is exactly those blocks. In the frontispiece's notation the demand holds at β₃₂ while the mantissa slips 65535 → 55402: the first adjustment lives entirely inside the target's mantissa, below the resolution of a zero-bit count.
