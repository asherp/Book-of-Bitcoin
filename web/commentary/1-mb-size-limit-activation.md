Satoshi's quiet cap: from height 79,400 a block may not exceed 1,000,000 bytes. Nothing on this page marks it — the first height-flagged soft fork (12 September 2010) was announced almost nowhere, and an activation chapter shows only what every chapter shows. What it set running is visible in every chapter after it: each one's length is written under this ceiling, and the seven years of argument the sentence seeded — the block size wars — were about how long a chapter may be, and who gets to write it.
