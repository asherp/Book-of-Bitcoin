Another round number — but this coinbase writes it out. Since BIP34 a coinbase must open with the height of its own block, so the first line of this section, under ■, says 500,000 in the miner's own hand: the page states the milestone rather than the reader counting to it.

The rest of the passage shows the month it landed in. December 2017 was when the queue for block space grew long enough that a fee stopped being a rounding error and became a bid — and it is on this page, in the coinbase's balance line, that the reward stands visibly above the 12.5 coins the schedule grants: the difference is that month's fee market, summed into one output. Everyone who had spent the previous seven years arguing about a limit found out that month what one feels like, and this section is what it looks like.
