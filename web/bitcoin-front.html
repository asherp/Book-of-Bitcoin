The reference

A citation names a place in the chain, one level at a time. Each level carries the sign of what it is:

III
Volume
an era of supply, opened by a halving
β2
Book
a difficulty window, β its mark
■5
Chapter
a block, ■ its mark
§1
Section
a transaction within the block

Read aloud: the fifth block of the second difficulty window of the third halving era, first transaction. The volume is set in Roman numerals, as a book's volumes are. A citation may stop early — III β2 names the whole book, and III β2 ■5 the whole chapter.
